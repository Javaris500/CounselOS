CounselOS · Design System · v5
Paper & Ink, on a real foundation
Bone paper replaces beige. Cool ink replaces brown. Every measurement now comes off a 4pt grid, every text size off an eight-step scale, every interactive element off a defined state matrix, and every surface off a five-layer elevation model. The concept is unchanged — the execution is now buildable.
01
Bone, not beige
Chroma cut roughly 70%. Still warm against browser white; no longer amber in screenshots.
02
Cool ink on warm stock
The print pairing. Removes the khaki cast from every piece of metadata in the product.
03
4pt grid, 8 type sizes
Replaces forty-odd hand-picked odd numbers and sixteen unrelated font sizes.
04
One urgency ladder
Slate → amber → terracotta → crimson, each a clear step hotter, and never hue alone.
05
Square-ish geometry
Pills retired. Five radii, assigned by element size. Instruments have corners.
06
Newsreader for headings
Holds its shape at 16–18px, where DM Serif Display went blunt. Never set bold.
07
Lucide, not dingbats
☎ ✉ ⚇ rendered differently on every machine. Now 16px strokes inheriting ink.
08
Selected is a state
Rows, cards and cells get rest, hover, active, focus, selected, disabled and loading.

1.11 · Motion — four durations, one philosophy
80ms · ease-out
Tint
Hover washes, border shifts. Fast enough to feel like the cursor, not an animation.
140ms · ease-out
State
Selection, checkbox, tab underline, focus ring. Confirms an action landed.
240ms · decelerate
Enter
Drawers, modals, menus. Objects arriving carry weight; leaving takes 160ms.
320ms · settle
Reconcile
An optimistic row confirming against the server. The only motion a user may notice.
The philosophy: motion reports state, it never decorates. Nothing eases in on scroll, nothing bounces, nothing staggers. Every animation in this system is honoured by prefers-reduced-motion — including the streaming caret and skeleton pulse, which v4 left unguarded.

Focus is a 2px slate-800 outline at 2px offset — a 5.2:1 ring against paper. v4 focused with slate-500, which cleared only 4.4:1 and failed the 3:1 non-text minimum with no margin.

Burst — the event feed collapses

SSE arrives in bursts. Three documents finishing together produce one toast with a count and a single action, stacked over its siblings — never three toasts racing each other off the screen.
A critical toast carries an action and no countdown. If a toast can expire unread it must not be the only place the information lives — which is why critical ones don't.
Titles carry the outcome, not the verb: "1.5 hours logged", not "Success". The matter name is always on the second line, because a toast without a matter is unactionable in a firm running forty of them.
Maximum three visible at once, newest at the bottom, hovering the stack pauses every countdown. Anything that scrolls out unread lands in the notification bell — the toast is a courtesy, never the record.

Part six
Mobile
The two surfaces have opposite mobile needs. The client page is read on a phone by default — a text message with a link, opened in a parking lot. The attorney side is desktop-primary but has exactly two things that matter on a phone: reading today's deadlines, and logging a call the moment it ends.
6.1 · Breakpoints, touch and the mobile type ramp
Phone
< 600px
Single column. Sidebar becomes a four-item bottom bar. Every drawer becomes a bottom sheet. Comfortable density everywhere, including the attorney side — density is a mouse affordance, not a thumb one. Tables become stacked rows or are simply not offered.
Tablet
600 – 1023px
Sidebar collapses to a 56px icon rail with labels on long-press. Two-column content maximum. Compact density returns, but hit targets stay at 44px. Kanban scrolls horizontally, one column visible at a time.
Desktop
≥ 1024px
Full 148px sidebar, compact density, side drawers, everything in Parts three to five as drawn.
Touch rules
44
Minimum target 44×44, even when the visible control is smaller. A 17px checkbox gets a 44px tap area.
At least 8px between adjacent targets — the row of six communication-type cells is the tightest thing in the product and it still clears this.
Primary actions live in the bottom third of the screen. Sheet confirmations are full-width and thumb-reachable; nothing important sits in a top corner.
No hover-only affordance ever carries meaning. Every row action visible on desktop hover is a visible control or a swipe on touch.
Inputs set at 16px minimum on phone — anything smaller makes iOS Safari zoom the viewport on focus.

6.2 · Mobile geometry — the phone gets its own scale
Radius scales with touch, not with brand. A 56px target needs a proportional corner or it reads as a stretched desktop control. Desktop stays at 4 / 6 / 8 because it is mouse-scale and dense; the phone goes soft, borderless and pill-shaped.
Fields — pill, filled, borderless
Inset fill on paper, no border, 52px tall, 16px text. The well is the affordance.
Buttons — full pill, 56px
Full width in sheets and at page end. Secondary is a filled well, never an outline.
Cards — 18px, no border
Depth comes from a soft double shadow. Hairlines are a desktop device.
Sheets — 24px top corners
Bottom-anchored, grab handle, 20px safe-area padding below the last control.
Gutters go to 24px and the gap between blocks to 32px. The client page pushes to 28 / 40 — feeling unhurried is its entire job.
One surface, one shadow. Cards sit on paper with no border and a two-part shadow: a 1px contact shadow plus a wide soft one. Nothing on mobile uses a hairline to separate itself from the page.
No gradients, no glass, no accent fills. The richness comes from space, radius, weight and depth. The moment a legal instrument starts looking like a consumer app, it stops being trusted.
